# The Carton Already Printed It

Entry for [Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-fridge-roast-one-line-biggest-laugh-a7c354bb) — one line roasting a smart fridge, judged head to head by an AI judge.

## The roast

> My smart fridge's proudest feature is texting me my milk expires tomorrow — $3,000 of Wi-Fi and touchscreens to do what the carton already printed.

147 characters. The joke aims squarely at the smart fridge itself — its app, its Wi-Fi, its touchscreen — and turns on the gap between the price tag and the "feature": a $3,000 appliance whose proudest trick is reading a date the carton already printed for free. It dodges the obvious cracks (no grocery ordering, no spying camera) and lands its punchline in the final clause, with every word earning its place.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the roast, deterministically, to `/output/entry.txt`. No input, no network, no randomness — two runs produce byte-identical output.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
